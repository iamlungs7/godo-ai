GODO AI CORE

Artificial Intelligence Core

The intelligence layer behind the GODO AI trading platform. It processes market information, evaluates trading conditions, and helps transform market data into structured trading signals.

🧠

Signal Intelligence

Analyses market conditions and identifies potential trading opportunities using structured market signals.

📊

Market Detection

Monitors supported markets and evaluates price behaviour, market structure and technical conditions.

🛡️

Risk Logic

Structures entry zones, stop-loss levels, take-profit targets and risk-to-reward information for generated signals.

Market Data → Analysis → Signal → Validation → Active Signal → Execution Layer.
